What is Bond?

Bond is a social wellness experience platform based in Toronto. We create curated outdoor events that blend movement, connection, and lifestyle.

The Experience

  • Move — fitness, hikes, outdoor activity
  • Meet — real connection in small groups
  • Vibe — music, nature, curated environments

Who it's for

  • 22–32 year olds in Toronto
  • People who want real-life connection
  • Health, fitness, and wellness focused lifestyle
  • People seeking better social experiences
