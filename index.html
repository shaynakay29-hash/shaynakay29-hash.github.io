What is Bond?

Bond is a social wellness experience platform based in Toronto. We create curated outdoor events that blend movement, connection, and lifestyle. Not networking. Not dating. Just better ways to meet people.

The Experience

Move

Outdoor fitness, hikes, seasonal wellness experiences that feel alive.

Meet

Small-group environments designed for real connection—not networking.

Vibe

Music, golden-hour settings, and curated social energy in nature.

Who it's for

  • 22–32 year olds in Toronto
  • People who want real-life connection
  • Health, fitness, and wellness focused lifestyle
  • People seeking better social experiences
